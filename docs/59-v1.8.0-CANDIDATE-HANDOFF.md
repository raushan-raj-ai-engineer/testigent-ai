## Evidence/claim discipline

Do not publish benchmark savings or scale claims from the example templates or synthetic planner. Real claims require measured data with methodology, commit and raw-evidence SHA-256. Until then the product reports `INSUFFICIENT_EVIDENCE`.

## Candidate rule

Do not tag `v1.8.0` merely because the ZIP's static/offline checks pass. PR/main/rerun and full Release Compatibility certification remain mandatory.

## Additional benchmark evidence workflow

- `npm run qa:false-heal -- --input=<measured-false-heal-evidence.json>` persists immutable seeded-defect evidence and updates benchmark intelligence.
- Comparative results are isolated by dataset version, baseline label and metric; unrelated benchmark campaigns are never aggregated into one claim.
- Synthetic/template or incomplete-provenance evidence remains non-certifying.
