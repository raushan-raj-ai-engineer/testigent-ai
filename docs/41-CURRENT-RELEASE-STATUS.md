## Evidence and claim discipline

Certification of the framework release does not automatically certify customer-adoption, productivity, differentiation or large-scale throughput claims.

Those claims remain dependent on real measured evidence and required provenance.

Synthetic examples, templates and planning diagnostics remain non-certifying. Sparse, synthetic or incomplete observations must continue to surface as insufficient evidence rather than being promoted into product or market claims.
